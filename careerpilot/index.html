CareerPilot

CareerPilot is a privately operated, personal job-search assistant developed by Tom Reilly.

It is designed to help organise job opportunities, process job-alert emails and support job-search workflows.

Google Integration

CareerPilot uses authorised Google account access to process relevant job-alert emails. Access is granted by the account owner through Google OAuth.

The associated Google Cloud project also supports a separate personal Google Drive connection used for file management.
